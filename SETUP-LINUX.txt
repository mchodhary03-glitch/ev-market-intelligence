EV INSIGHTS WEBSITE — QUICK START

1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open a terminal in ev-market-intelligence-website.
3. Run: python3 -m http.server 8000
4. Visit: http://localhost:8000
5. Edit config.js to add your Tableau Public view URL and Google Drive video share URL.
6. For free public hosting, follow README.md > Free hosting with GitHub Pages.
