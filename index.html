THREE STAKEHOLDER SCENARIOS

One dataset. Three perspectives.

The dashboard is designed to help each stakeholder investigate a different set of business and research questions.

01 / STRATEGY

Emily Johnson

EV Market Strategy Director

Identify high-performing vehicle categories, leading manufacturers, customer satisfaction patterns and promising battery technologies.

  • Which categories lead in sales?
  • How does range vary by segment?
  • Where are growth opportunities?
02 / BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE

David Chen

Automotive BI Manager

Compare manufacturer performance and production geography to support market-entry and positioning decisions.

  • How is sales performance distributed?
  • Which battery types are common?
  • Which countries manufacture the vehicles?
03 / SUSTAINABILITY

Dr. Sarah Williams

Sustainable Mobility Research Analyst

Investigate battery innovation, driving range, customer adoption indicators and reported environmental impact.

  • How do battery types compare?
  • What range/capacity patterns appear?
  • How do sustainability indicators vary?
